# Service desk

The IT service desk is staffed Monday to Friday, 08:00-18:00 MYT. Outside these hours, only critical incidents are handled by the on-call engineer.

Support tickets are raised through the assistant or the service desk portal. Every ticket needs a short title, a severity and enough detail to reproduce the problem.

# Severity levels

- Critical: a production service is down or customer data is at risk. Response within 15 minutes, around the clock.
- High: a production service is degraded, for example repeated API timeouts. Response within 1 hour during service desk hours.
- Medium: a non-production system or a single team is affected. Response within 1 business day.
- Low: questions and minor issues. Response within 3 business days.
