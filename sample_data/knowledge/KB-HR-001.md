title: Annual Leave Policy
department: hr
classification: internal
updated_at: 2026-07-01
---

Employees receive 14 days of annual leave after confirmation. Requests of three or more consecutive days should be submitted at least seven calendar days in advance. Managers may approve exceptions for emergencies. Unused leave may be carried forward up to five days and must be used by 31 March of the following year.
