VPN Access Policy

Remote access to internal systems requires a personal OpenVPN profile.

A VPN profile is created by IT Operations only after an authorized approver (a holder of the vpn:approve permission) confirms the request. The requester and the approver must be different people.

Profiles are valid for at most 30 days. Expired profiles are revoked automatically and must be requested again.

Shared or generic VPN profiles are not permitted. Lost devices must be reported to the service desk immediately so the profile can be revoked.
